- (event) session started: create product brief
- (direction) step-by-step coaching discovery path chosen
- (concept) Debit Recovery System: track and recover outstanding balances
- (users) Administrators and recovery officers
- (target-orgs) Businesses, lending organizations, financial departments
- (core-capabilities) Customer registration, debt records, payment deadlines, payment recording, activity tracking, recovery analytics and dashboards
- (research) domain patterns captured: aging buckets (30/60/90+ DPD), PTP (Promise-to-Pay) tracking, audit trail compliance, supervisor queues
- (pain) Spreadsheets and manual tracking lead to missed deadlines, lost customer history, and forgotten balances
- (platform) Mobile-first app (iOS & Android via Flutter) primarily empowering recovery officers
- (business-model) Multi-tenant SaaS platform allowing multiple business/lender subscribers to manage debtors
- (workflow) Prioritized daily task list sorted by urgency/aging (DPD), quick actions (Call, Log Visit, Record Payment), Promise-to-Pay (PTP) reminders
- (differentiator) Simplicity, speed, and affordability: mobile-first SaaS designed for small-to-midsize lenders without legacy enterprise bloat
- (scope-in) Tenant organization onboarding, Customer/Debtor registry, Debt creation & aging tracking, Officer assignment, Activity logging (calls/visits), Payment recording & receipts, Analytics dashboard
- (scope-out) Automated legal filing, complex court proceedings, direct bank auto-debit sweeps, automated dialers (deferred to later releases)
