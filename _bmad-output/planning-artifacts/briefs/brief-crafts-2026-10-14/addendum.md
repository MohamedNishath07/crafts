# Addendum: Domain Research & Reference Data

## Domain Analysis: Modern Debt Recovery & Collection Systems

*Source: Subagent domain research (Oct 2026)*

### 1. Key Operational Roles
- **System Admin / Manager:** RBAC, ERP/billing integration, compliance rules, contact cadence configuration.
- **Credit Manager / Supervisor:** Portfolio aging analysis, collector targets, queue assignment, settlement/write-off approvals.
- **Recovery / Collection Officer (Desk or Field):** Daily task queues, interaction logging, field visits/geolocation, Promise-to-Pay (PTP) negotiation.
- **Customer / Debtor:** Payment options, statement viewing, dispute submission, installment requests.

### 2. Standard Workflow & State Machine
1. **Debt Ingestion & Segmentation:** Dynamic aging buckets (Current, 30, 60, 90, 120+ DPD - Days Past Due), risk scoring.
2. **Activity & Contact Cadence:** Omnichannel logs (calls, visits, emails, SMS, letters).
3. **Promise-to-Pay (PTP) Tracking:** Commitment dates, promised amounts, automatic reminders, broken-promise escalation.
4. **Payment & Partial Reconciliation:** Handling full settlement vs. installment plans vs. write-offs.
5. **Escalation / Legal Handoff:** Routing delinquent accounts to recovery legal teams or external agencies.

### 3. Critical System Requirements
- Clear audit trails for regulatory compliance (preventing harassment or contact outside legal windows).
- Instant status updates when payments are recorded so recovery officers do not pursue settled debts.
